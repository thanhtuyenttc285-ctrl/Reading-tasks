Preparing a Wire for Connection

Nam is a trainee electrician. Today, he is preparing a wire for a connection in the electrical workshop. He needs three tools: wire strippers, long nose pliers, and pressing pincers.

First, Nam uses wire strippers to remove the insulation from the end of the wire. He chooses the correct groove for the size of the wire. Then, he strips the insulation carefully.

Next, Nam uses long nose pliers. He holds the end of the wire with the pliers and bends it into the correct position for the connection.

Finally, Nam puts the wire into a terminal. He uses pressing pincers to press the terminal firmly onto the wire. He presses the metal part of the terminal, not the insulation.

Now the wire is ready for the connection. Using the right tool for each step helps Nam prepare the wire correctly.
